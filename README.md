US-01: Create a Product
As a Product Administrator, I want to create a product with its associated information so that it can be stored in the product catalog.

Acceptance criteria:
1. Product name is required and must follow length restrictions.
2. SKU is required, follows a defined format, and must be unique (case-insensitive).
3. Product price must be greater than zero and use a supported currency.
4. Product must belong to an existing, active category.
5. Brand must exist and be active, if specified.
6. Product weight and dimensions must be valid.
7. Product must have a unique identifier.
8. Newly created products must have Draft status.
